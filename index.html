About

A little about me

I'm a data analyst with an M.S. and a B.S. in Technological Systems Management from Stony Brook University, plus an Advanced Graduate Certificate in Data and Computational Science.

I like practical questions with messy data behind them: cleaning the data up, querying it, and turning it into charts and reports people can act on. As an MTA intern I automated the tracking of disciplinary files with Excel and Python. I've also analyzed how NYC traffic changed after congestion pricing began, and built a point-of-sale system that a convenience store uses every day to record its sales and produce its reports.

I'm looking for data analyst, BI, and SQL roles where I can help a team make better decisions with its data.
